The First 48 Directory

The First 48, from Crime House Studios and A&E, is built on a simple rule: a homicide detective’s chance of solving a murder is cut in half without a lead in the first 48 hours. Each episode drops you inside that critical window, following the interrogations, dead ends, and breakthroughs of a landmark investigation.

This directory covers 11 public episodes, 2026 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
