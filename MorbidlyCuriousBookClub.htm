Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Morbidly Curious Book Club, covering 71 public episodes from 2024 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Morbidly Curious Book Club Directory

A book club of morbidly curious nonfiction, with conversations with the authors of its picks.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 71 public episodes, 2024 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
