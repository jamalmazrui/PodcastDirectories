Abstract
Every available banquet address from the National Federation of the Blind national convention, from the 1940 founding convention to 2026, in one chronological sequence. Each speech links to its audio and its text. A few national convention addresses that are not banquet speeches are included where they carry a lasting philosophical statement of the movement and exist in audio.

National Federation of the Blind Banquet Addresses

The banquet address is the highlight of the National Federation of the Blind’s annual national convention. Delivered by the president at the convention banquet, it is where the organized blind movement sets out its philosophy, and it usually ends in a long standing ovation. This directory gathers every banquet address the NFB has made available, from the 1940 founding convention through 2026.

The speeches run in one chronological sequence, grouped by decade, oldest first. Each speech’s title link plays its audio recording so it starts with one press; a “Read the text” link follows for the written version, and a “Watch the video” link appears for the few that were filmed. A speech with no recording links straight to its text instead. Below each title you will find its location, speaker, and year. Two appendixes list every speech again: by speaker, and by title.

The directory is mostly the annual banquet address. A few national convention addresses that are not banquet speeches are included as well, where the speech exists in audio, was given at the national convention, and stands as a lasting philosophical statement of the movement; each such entry says so in its note. Ordinary convention business addresses, memorial talks, and alternate radio versions are left out.
