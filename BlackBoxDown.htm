Black Box Down Directory

Black Box Down breaks down major aviation accidents and the safety changes they led to, drawing on official investigation reports.

This directory covers 150 public episodes, 2020 to 2023. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
