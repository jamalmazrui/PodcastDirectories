A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast In the Dark, covering 23 public episodes from 2016 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

In the Dark Directory

In the Dark, hosted by Madeleine Baran, is an award-winning investigative-journalism series, now produced by The New Yorker after years at American Public Media. Each season spends a year or more on a single case, from the Jacob Wetterling abduction to the six trials of Curtis Flowers to the killings at Haditha, which won the Pulitzer Prize for audio.

This directory covers 23 public episodes, 2016 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.

2018 (2)

Season 2, Episode 1: July 16, 1996

Date: May 1, 2018

Duration: 2737

Episode: 1

Season: 2

Summary: On the morning of July 16, 1996, someone walked into a furniture store in downtown Winona, Mississippi, and murdered four employees. Each was shot in the head. It was perhaps the most shocking crime the small town had ever seen. Investigators charged a man named Curtis Flowers with the murders. What followed was a two-decade legal odyssey in which Flowers was tried six times for the same crime. He remains on death row, though some people believe he’s innocent. For the second season of In the Dark, we spent a year digging into the Flowers case. We found a town divided by race and a murder conviction supported by questionable evidence. And it all began that summer morning in 1996 with a horrifying crime scene that left investigators puzzled
