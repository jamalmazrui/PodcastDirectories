Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast True Crime Story: It Couldn’t Happen Here, covering 25 public episodes from 2023 to 2024. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

True Crime Story: It Couldn’t Happen Here Directory

Actor and advocate Hilarie Burton Morgan and the crew of SundanceTV’s documentary series go deeper into its cases: violent crimes in small towns, and the places where the justice system seems to have failed.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 25 public episodes, 2023 to 2024. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
