American Elections: Wicked Game Directory

American Elections: Wicked Game, from Airship and Wondery, tells the story of every United States presidential election, from the unanimous choice of George Washington in 1789 to the 2024 contest between Donald Trump and Kamala Harris. It is hosted by Lindsay Graham, the host of American History Tellers and American Scandal. Its premise is that there never was a “good old days”: presidential politics has always been played rough.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 64 public episodes, 2023 to 2024. Each episode’s title link plays its audio, and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Every episode is in season 1, so that field is left out. Summaries drop the feed’s repeated advertising notes. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; all sorting ignores case.

The show began in 2019 and was republished from the start in November 2023, one election a week, so every publication date falls in 2023 or 2024. Those dates say little about the subject, so the 61 election episodes are grouped instead by era of American history, in election order, with the trailers and announcements in a section of their own. Appendixes reorder all episodes by date (newest first) and by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.

Reconstruction and the Gilded Age, 1868 to 1896 (8)

Date: February 6, 2024

Duration: 00:48:47

Episode: 21

Summary: The death of Abraham Lincoln sets the stage for one of the greatest political showdowns in American history.
