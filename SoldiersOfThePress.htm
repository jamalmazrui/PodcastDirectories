Soldiers of the Press

Dramatized reports from United Press war correspondents during World War Two.

  • Episodes: 40
  • Network: Syndicated
  • Source: Internet Archive: OTRR_Soldiers_Of_The_Press_Singles
  • Years: 1942 to 1945

Each episode title is a link that plays its audio. These broadcasts can reflect the attitudes of their time. This page is part of Old Time Radio Dramas.
