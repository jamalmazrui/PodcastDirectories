A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast The Constant, covering 242 public episodes from 2017 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

The Constant Directory

The Constant: A History of Getting Things Wrong, hosted by Mark Chrisler, examines the bad ideas, mistakes, and accidents that misshaped our world, a history of human error told with curiosity and humor.

This directory covers 242 public episodes, 2017 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
