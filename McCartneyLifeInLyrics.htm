A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast McCartney: A Life in Lyrics, covering 26 public episodes from 2023 to 2024. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

McCartney: A Life in Lyrics Directory

Paul McCartney and the poet Paul Muldoon talk through the people, places and experiences behind his songs, from the Beatles and Wings to his solo work, in conversations recorded for their book The Lyrics.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 26 public episodes, 2023 to 2024. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
