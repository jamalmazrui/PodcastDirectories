Box 13

Alan Ladd plays a writer who answers ads sent to a newspaper box, looking for adventure and story ideas.

  • Episodes: 51
  • Network: Syndicated
  • Source: Internet Archive: OTRR_Box_13_Singles
  • Years: 1948 to 1949

Each episode title is a link that plays its audio. These broadcasts can reflect the attitudes of their time. This page is part of Old Time Radio Dramas.
