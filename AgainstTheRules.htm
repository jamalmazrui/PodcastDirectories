Against the Rules Directory

Against the Rules, hosted by journalist and best-selling author Michael Lewis (Moneyball, The Big Short) for Pushkin, examines the people in American life who are supposed to be fair, from referees to coaches to experts, and asks what happens when we stop trusting them.

This directory covers 107 public episodes, 2019 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
