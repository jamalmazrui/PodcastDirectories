Gladiator: Aaron Hernandez and Football Inc. Directory

The Boston Globe and Wondery trace how Aaron Hernandez went from a star of the New England Patriots to a convicted murderer, and what football did to him along the way.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 2 public episodes, 2018 to 2018. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.
