Conversations with Coleman Directory

Conversations with Coleman, hosted by writer Coleman Hughes, is a series of in-depth interviews on politics, philosophy, race, culture, and science.

This directory covers 257 public episodes, 2019 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Guests, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title, and by guest.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
