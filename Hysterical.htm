Hysterical Directory

Hysterical, hosted by Dan Taberski for Wondery and Pineapple Street Studios, investigates a mysterious illness that spread among high school girls in Le Roy, New York, in 2011.

This directory covers 2 public episodes, 2024. Each episode’s title link plays its audio, and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; all sorting ignores case.

Only the episodes in the show’s public feed are listed. Wondery’s narrative shows moved to Audible in 2025, and for several of them the public feed now keeps only part of the catalog; the rest is available to Audible subscribers.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

For current details, the show’s own pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.

Introducing: Hysterical

Summary: Hysterical investigates a mysterious illness that spreads among a group of high school girls in upstate New York. What is causing their sudden, often violent symptoms?
