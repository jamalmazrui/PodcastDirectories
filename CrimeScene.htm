Crime Scene Directory

Crime Scene, from The Binge, is a weekly true-crime show in which Jonathan Hirsch and investigative reporter Cooper Moll revisit cases that deserve a second look.

This directory covers 27 public episodes, 2026 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.

The Boys on the Tracks

Date: March 26, 2026

Duration: 58:48

Episode: 1

Season: 1

Summary: Two teens are found dead on railroad tracks. The official ruling? An accident. But the scene tells a different story. In 1987, two Arkansas teenagers were found dead on a set of railroad tracks under circumstances that defied easy explanation. Despite unusual evidence at the scene–including a destroyed firearm and reports of missing materials–their deaths were officially ruled an accident. In this episode, we examine the “Boys on the Tracks” case, unpacking the inconsistencies, investigative missteps, and lingering questions that have fueled decades of speculation. As the timeline unfolds, key details begin to challenge the official narrative and raise the possibility that the full story was never uncovered.
