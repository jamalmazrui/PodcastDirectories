Deep Cover Directory

Deep Cover, from Pushkin and hosted by Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist Jake Halpern, investigates people who lead double lives, told across multi-part seasons.

This directory covers 84 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
