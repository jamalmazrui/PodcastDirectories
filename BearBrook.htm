Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Bear Brook, covering 22 public episodes from 2018 to 2025. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Bear Brook Directory

Bear Brook, hosted by Jason Moon for New Hampshire Public Radio, is a critically acclaimed true crime series. Season 1 follows the decades-old cold case of four bodies found in barrels in the New Hampshire woods and the forensic genealogy that cracked it; Season 2 questions a murder conviction built on a taped confession.

This directory covers 22 public episodes, 2018 to 2025. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
