Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast The Invisible Network, covering 39 public episodes from 2018 to 2022. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

The Invisible Network Directory

The Invisible Network, from NASA, tells the story of the often-overlooked technologies that let NASA talk to and navigate its spacecraft, from laser communications to the Deep Space Network. It was NASA’s first podcast built on narrative storytelling.

This directory covers 39 public episodes, 2018 to 2022. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
