Commonwealth Confidential Directory

Commonwealth Confidential, from NBC10 Boston, gives nightly coverage of major Massachusetts trials, each trial a season. Recent seasons cover the Lindsay Clancy, Karen Read, Brian Walshe, Kelsey Fitzsimmons, and Stefon Diggs cases.

This directory covers 180 public episodes, 2024 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
