The Poetry of Reality Directory

The Poetry of Reality is hosted by evolutionary biologist and author Richard Dawkins, who explores science, evolution, and reason, often in conversation with a guest and sometimes reacting to clips from around the internet.

This directory is built from the show’s RSS feed and covers its public episodes. Because the feed does not provide a separate web page for each episode, every episode links to its audio file, which plays or downloads the episode directly.

Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order, each shown only when available. Values inside a field are sorted alphabetically, ignoring case; titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”, and people are sorted by surname.

Episodes are grouped into the categories below, and within each they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Three appendixes offer other orders: by date, by title, and by guest.

The show is the work of Richard Dawkins and his team; for current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the public feed.
