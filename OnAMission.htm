Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast On a Mission, covering 45 public episodes from 2018 to 2023. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

On a Mission Directory

On a Mission, from NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory, is a narrative podcast that follows a single space mission at a time through the people who make it happen, from the InSight lander’s journey to Mars to asteroid missions and Mars rovers.

This directory covers 45 public episodes, 2018 to 2023. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
