Kingdom of Fraud Directory

Kingdom of Fraud, from Novel, has investigative reporter Michele McPhee trace the billion-dollar tax-fraud conspiracy linking a Utah polygamist clan and a Los Angeles tycoon.

This directory covers 12 public episodes, 2026 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
