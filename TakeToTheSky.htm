Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Take to the Sky: The Air Disaster Podcast, covering 100 public episodes from 2021 to 2023. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Take to the Sky Directory

Take to the Sky, hosted by Shelly Price and Stephanie Hubka, examines the facts and the human stories behind aviation disasters.

This directory covers 100 public episodes, 2021 to 2023. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
