Legal Wars Directory

Legal Wars, from Wondery, puts you inside the jury box of some of the most famous court cases in American history, from Hulk Hogan’s fight with Gawker to the Rodney King trial. Hosted by actor and Harvard Law graduate Hill Harper, it is built on interviews and court transcripts. Note: this directory reflects the free public feed, which now carries only a few episodes; the fuller run is on Wondery+.

This directory covers 4 public episodes, 2018 to 2018. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
