Sword and Scale Directory

Sword and Scale is a weekly true-crime podcast, hosted by Mike Boudet, covering crime and the criminal-justice system through raw audio: 911 calls, court testimony, and interviews. The first episode aired in 2014.

This directory is built from the show’s public RSS feed, which carries the more recent seasons rather than the entire back catalog, so it is a guide to the currently public episodes rather than every episode ever made. Premium and +PLUS episodes are not included.

Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order, and every field appears only when it is available. Values inside a field are sorted alphabetically, ignoring case; titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”, and people are sorted by surname.

Episodes are grouped into the categories below and, within each, listed in order by title. Two appendixes offer other orders: by date and by title. A note on content: Sword and Scale covers graphic violent crime and is intended for adult listeners.

The show is the work of Mike Boudet and Sword and Scale; for current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the public feed.
