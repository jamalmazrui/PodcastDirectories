Freedom Scientific FSCast Directory

FSCast is the official podcast of Freedom Scientific, hosted by Glen Gordon, with news, interviews, and product demonstrations for JAWS, ZoomText, and Fusion.

This directory covers 272 public episodes, 2006 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Guests, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title, and by guest.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
