National Federation of the Blind Presidential Reports

Each year at the national convention, the president of the National Federation of the Blind delivers the Presidential Report: a full account of the movement’s work over the past year. The NFB treats these reports as its official historical record, and they gather in one place the honors won, the laws and lawsuits fought, the new programs begun, and the technology advanced. This directory collects every Presidential Report the NFB has put online, from 1987 through 2026.

The reports run in one chronological sequence, grouped by decade, oldest first. Each report’s title link plays its audio recording where one exists, so it starts with one press, and a “Read the text” link follows for the written version; a “Watch the video” link appears for the two that were filmed. Reports before 2006 are text only and link straight to their text. Below each title you will find its date, location, and speaker. An appendix lists every report again by speaker.

The NFB publishes presidential reports online beginning with 1987, the first full year of Marc Maurer’s presidency; earlier reports are not part of the online archive.
