Gravity Assist Directory

Gravity Assist, from NASA, is an interplanetary talk show hosted by former NASA Chief Scientist Jim Green. Across five seasons he interviews leading planetary scientists for a guided tour of the solar system and beyond. The series concluded in 2022.

This directory covers 98 public episodes, 2017 to 2022. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
