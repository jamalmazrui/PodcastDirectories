Richard Durham’s dramas about the lives of real Black Americans.

  • Episodes: 102
  • Network: WMAQ Chicago
  • Source: Internet Archive: OTRR_Destination_Freedom_Singles
  • Years: 1948 to 1950

Each episode title is a link that plays its audio. These broadcasts can reflect the attitudes of their time. This page is part of Old Time Radio Dramas.
