Revisionist History Directory

Revisionist History, hosted by Malcolm Gladwell for Pushkin, is his journey through the overlooked and the misunderstood, re-examining an event, a person, an idea, even a song, and asking whether we got it right the first time.

This directory covers 213 public episodes, 2016 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
