Unsolved Mysteries Directory

The official Unsolved Mysteries podcast, from the creators of the television series and presented by Audacy, presents new cases of unexplained disappearances, deaths, and mysteries.

This directory covers 91 public episodes, 2021 to 2024. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
