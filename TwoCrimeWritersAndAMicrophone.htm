Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Two Crime Writers and a Microphone, covering 128 public episodes from 2016 to 2023. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Two Crime Writers and a Microphone Directory

Thriller writers Steve Cavanagh and Luca Veste talk about books and publishing, and speak with the biggest names in crime fiction.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 128 public episodes, 2016 to 2023. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
