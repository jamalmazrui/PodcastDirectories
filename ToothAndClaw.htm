Tooth & Claw Directory

Tooth & Claw, hosted by wildlife biologist Wes Larson with Jeff Larson and Mike Smith, recounts true wild-animal attacks and explains the animal behavior behind them and how to stay safe.

This directory covers 212 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
