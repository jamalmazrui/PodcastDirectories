21st Precinct

Realistic dramas about one New York City police precinct, built on how real officers worked.

  • Episodes: 107
  • Network: CBS
  • Source: Internet Archive: OTRR_21st_Precinct_Singles
  • Years: 1953 to 1956

Each episode title is a link that plays its audio. These broadcasts can reflect the attitudes of their time. This page is part of Old Time Radio Dramas.
