Sidedoor Directory

Sidedoor is the Smithsonian’s podcast, sneaking listeners through the side door to tell stories about science, art, history, and humanity from beyond the public view.

This directory covers 254 public episodes, 2016 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Guests, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title, and by guest.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.

Dark Matter, Bright Mind: How Vera Rubin Saw the Unseen

Summary: Something dark and invisible makes up as much as 90 to 95 percent of the universe—and it took a little girl staring out a bedroom window at the night sky to bring it to light.
