Real Survival Stories Directory

Real Survival Stories, hosted by John Hopkins for Noiser, recounts the true experiences of ordinary people thrust into extraordinary survival situations.

This directory covers 171 public episodes, 2023 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
