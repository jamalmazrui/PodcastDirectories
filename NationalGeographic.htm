Overheard at National Geographic Directory

Overheard at National Geographic follows explorers, photographers, and scientists to the edges of the world, drawn from conversations overheard at National Geographic’s headquarters.

This directory covers 160 public episodes, 2019 to 2023. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Guests, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title, and by guest.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
