Counterspy

Agents of a counterspy agency track down foreign spies, saboteurs and smugglers.

  • Episodes: 71
  • Network: ABC, NBC and Mutual
  • Source: Internet Archive: OTRR_Counterspy_Singles
  • Years: 1942 to 1957

Each episode title is a link that plays its audio. These broadcasts can reflect the attitudes of their time. This page is part of Old Time Radio Dramas.
