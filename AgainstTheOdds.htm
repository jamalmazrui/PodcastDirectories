Against the Odds Directory

Against the Odds, from Wondery, tells immersive true stories of survival and rescue, from cave rescues to hostage ordeals, in multi-part seasons.

This directory covers 288 public episodes, 2021 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
