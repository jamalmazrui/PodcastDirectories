A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Park Predators, covering 179 public episodes from 2020 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Park Predators Directory

Park Predators, hosted by investigative journalist Delia D’Ambra for Audiochuck, explores real crimes that happened in national and state parks and other wild places.

This directory covers 179 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
