Burden of Proof Directory

Burden of Proof, from ABC News and 20/20, is a narrative true-crime series hosted by legal analyst Brian Buckmire. Its seasons examine a single high-profile case, including Bad Rap: The Case Against Diddy and the Luigi Mangione trial.

This directory covers 37 public episodes, 2025 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
