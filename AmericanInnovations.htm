American Innovations Directory

American Innovations, hosted by best-selling author Steven Johnson (How We Got to Now) for Wondery, uses immersive scenes to tell the stories of the scientists, engineers, and ordinary people behind the great discoveries of the past century.

This directory covers 49 public episodes, 2018 to 2021. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
