Jamal Mazrui

Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Felonious Florida, covering 43 public episodes from 2018 to 2025. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Felonious Florida Directory

Wondery and the South Florida Sun Sentinel tell the stories of some of Florida’s most notorious crimes, beyond the beaches and theme parks.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 43 public episodes, 2018 to 2025. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
