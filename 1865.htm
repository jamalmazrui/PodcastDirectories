Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast 1865, covering 25 public episodes from 2019 to 2021. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

1865 Directory

A historical political thriller, with a full cast, about Secretary of War Edwin Stanton after Lincoln’s murder: the hunt for John Wilkes Booth, the fight with President Andrew Johnson, and the struggle over Reconstruction. Every story is based on true events.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 25 public episodes, 2019 to 2021. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
