Announcing Podcast Directories Single-Page Guides to 139 Podcasts, Radio Dramas and Audio Collections, Built for Screen Readers

https://github.com/JamalMazrui/PodcastDirectories/archive/main.zip

I am pleased to share Podcast Directories, a free set of web pages, one per show, that list every public episode with its date, length, and a short summary.

Why a single page instead of an app or a website? An app makes you scroll and tap through episodes a screen at a time, and a show’s website buries them in menus, players, and ads. A directory page holds the whole show in one file you can read straight through, move through by heading, search all at once with your browser’s find command, and open any episode with a link that plays it directly. No sign-in is needed.

Each page opens with a table of contents, groups episodes by season or year in date order, and also lists every episode by date, by title, and by guest or speaker.

This release has 139 directories: 114 podcasts and other audio collections with more than 35,000 episodes, 24 old time radio dramas with 4,909 episodes, and a catalog of more than 16,000 free audiobooks.

New in this release are classic radio dramas from the 1930s to the 1960s, chosen for true stories or realistic people and places, with no comedies and no science fiction, fantasy or supernatural tales. An Old Time Radio Dramas page groups them by kind.
