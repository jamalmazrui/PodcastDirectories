Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of Symphony of Science, the music video series by melodysheep, covering 25 videos from 2009 to 2019. Each video links to YouTube and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Symphony of Science Directory

Symphony of Science is a series of music videos by composer and filmmaker John D. Boswell, known as melodysheep. Each one remixes the recorded words of scientists and science communicators, such as Carl Sagan, Neil deGrasse Tyson, Richard Feynman and David Attenborough, into a song about a scientific subject, from the Big Bang to dinosaurs to climate change. The series aims to spread science and philosophy through music. Because the singing is the scientists’ own voices, tuned to the melody, the videos work well by ear. Many have the song’s lyrics in the YouTube description.

The videos and their music belong to their creator, melodysheep. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 25 videos, 2009 to 2019. Each video’s title link opens it on YouTube, and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Featuring, Installment, and Summary, each shown only when available. The installment number is the place in the series that the creator gave the video; the shorter specials and tributes have none. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Videos are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so the series reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title, and by the people featured.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. The official site is Symphony of Science, and the songs are also sold as albums on melodysheep’s Bandcamp page. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the melodysheep YouTube channel. The summaries are short restatements of each video’s own description.

2009 (3)

A Glorious Dawn

Date: September 17, 2009

Duration: 00:03:34

Featuring: Stephen Hawking, Carl Sagan

Installment: 1

Summary: The video that started the series: a musical tribute to Carl Sagan and Stephen Hawking, sampled almost entirely from Sagan’s Cosmos and Hawking’s Universe series.

We Are All Connected

Date: October 19, 2009

Duration: 00:04:12

Featuring: Richard Feynman, Bill Nye, Carl Sagan, Neil deGrasse Tyson

Installment: 2

Summary: A tribute to great minds of science, sampled from Cosmos, Richard Feynman’s 1983 interviews, Neil deGrasse Tyson and Bill Nye, about how we are made of star stuff.

Our Place in the Cosmos

Date: November 23, 2009

Duration: 00:04:20

Featuring: Richard Dawkins, Robert Jastrow, Michio Kaku, Carl Sagan

Installment: 3

Summary: Explores our origins within the universe and challenges the idea that humans hold a privileged place, on Earth or in the cosmos.

The Case for Mars

Date: June 3, 2010

Duration: 00:04:00

Featuring: Penelope Boston, Brian Cox, Carl Sagan, Robert Zubrin

Installment: 6

Summary: Meant to spark curiosity about Mars and promote human exploration of it, with samples from The Mars Underground, Cosmos and Wonders of the Solar System.

A Wave of Reason

Date: November 22, 2010

Duration: 00:03:51

Featuring: Richard Dawkins, Richard Feynman, Sam Harris, Lawrence Krauss, Phil Plait, Carolyn Porco, James Randi, Bertrand Russell, Carl Sagan, Michael Shermer

Installment: 7

Summary: Promotes scientific reasoning and skepticism in the face of pseudoscience such as astrology and homeopathy.
