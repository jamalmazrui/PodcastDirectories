The Disappearing Spoon Directory

The Disappearing Spoon, hosted by best-selling author Sam Kean and co-produced with the Science History Institute, tells overlooked stories from the history of science: the small, strange moments where the footnote turns out to be the real story.

This directory covers 138 public episodes, 2019 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
