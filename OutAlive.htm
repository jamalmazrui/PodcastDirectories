A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Out Alive, from Backpacker, covering 64 public episodes from 2019 to 2024. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Out Alive Directory

Out Alive, from Backpacker magazine, tells first-person stories of people who survived wilderness emergencies, with experts explaining what went wrong and how to stay safe.

This directory covers 64 public episodes, 2019 to 2024. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
