Abstract

A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast History Daily, covering 365 public episodes from 2025 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

History Daily Directory

History Daily, hosted by Lindsay Graham (American Scandal, American History Tellers) for Airship, Noiser, and Wondery, tells the true story of a momentous event that happened on this day in history, one day at a time.

This directory covers 365 public episodes, 2025 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
