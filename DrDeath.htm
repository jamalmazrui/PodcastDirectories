Dr. Death Directory

Dr. Death, from Wondery and hosted by journalist Laura Beil, investigates egregious cases of medical malpractice and the doctors behind them, across multi-part seasons.

This directory covers 17 public episodes, 2018 to 2026. Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season and, within each group, listed in order by title. Appendixes reorder them by date, by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
