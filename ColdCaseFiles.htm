Cold Case Files Directory

Cold Case Files, from A&E and PodcastOne, revisits some of the most difficult murders ever investigated, cases that stumped detectives and went cold, sometimes for decades, before a fresh lead, a confession, or new forensic science finally cracked them open.

This directory covers 225 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
