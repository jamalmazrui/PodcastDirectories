Access On Directory

Access On is the technology podcast of the National Federation of the Blind, hosted by Jonathan Mosen. It covers technology news, product demonstrations, reviews, and interviews, with a focus on nonvisual access. It began in late 2024 as the successor to Living Blindfully.

This directory is built from the show’s RSS feed and covers its public episodes, grouped by year.

Each episode links to its page and lists the metadata on record, one kind per field, in alphabetical order, and every field appears only when it is available. Values inside a field are sorted alphabetically, ignoring case; titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”, and people are sorted by surname.

Episodes are grouped into the categories below and, within each, listed in order by title. Two appendixes offer other orders: by date and by title.

The show is the work of Jonathan Mosen and the National Federation of the Blind; for current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the public feed.
