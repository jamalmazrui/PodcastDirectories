CounterClock Directory

CounterClock, hosted by investigative journalist Delia D’Ambra for Audiochuck, reopens a cold-case homicide each season, digging through evidence, records, and interviews to reignite interest in cases that went unsolved for years or decades.

This directory covers 129 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
