The Torch: The Great Courses Podcast Directory

Host Ed Leon meets the professors and experts who create The Great Courses, and hears their stories and insights. The series ended in 2017.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 70 public episodes, 2013 to 2017. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
