A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast Murder Shelf Book Club, covering 198 public episodes from 2020 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

Murder Shelf Book Club Directory

A true crime book club in podcast form: each episode works through a real case by way of a book about it.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 198 public episodes, 2020 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
