Announcing Podcast Directories

Announcing Podcast Directories A Set of Single-Page Guides to 52 Podcasts, Built for Screen Readers

Download the whole set at this web address: https://github.com/JamalMazrui/PodcastDirectories/archive/main.zip

I am pleased to share Podcast Directories, a free collection of web pages that make it easy to browse a podcast with a screen reader. There is one page for each show, and each page lists every public episode with its date, its length, and a short summary, plus the guests for interview shows.

Why a single page instead of an app or a website? In a podcast app you move through episodes a screen at a time, scrolling and tapping to find anything. A show’s website wraps that same information in menus, players, images, and ads. These pages remove the clutter and put the whole show in one file. You can read it straight through, jump around by heading, search all of it at once with your browser’s find command, and open any episode with a single link to its page or its audio. No sign-in is required.

The pages are organized for a screen reader. Each opens with a table of contents. Episodes are grouped by season or year at heading level 2, and each episode title is at heading level 3. The details sit in separate, alphabetically sorted fields, so a fact is always in the same place. Three appendixes let you find any episode by date, by title, or by guest.
