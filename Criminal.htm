Criminal Directory

Criminal, hosted by Phoebe Judge for the Vox Media Podcast Network, tells stories of people who have done wrong, been wronged, or gotten caught somewhere in the middle. It is known for empathy and craft over sensationalism.

This directory covers 405 public episodes, 2014 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
