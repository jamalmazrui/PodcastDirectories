A screen-reader-friendly directory of the podcast History That Doesn’t Suck, covering 240 public episodes from 2017 to 2026. Each episode links to its audio and lists its metadata in separate, alphabetically sorted fields.

History That Doesn’t Suck Directory

Professor Greg Jackson tells American history as researched, story-driven episodes, from the causes of the Revolution through the Civil War, the Gilded Age, Prohibition and the world wars.

Episode titles, summaries and audio belong to the show’s publisher. This directory’s own text and arrangement are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

This directory covers 240 public episodes, 2017 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, Season, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by year, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first) and by title.

This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
