The Binge Crimes Directory

The Binge Crimes is a true-crime feed from Sony Music Entertainment. It cycles through narrative series such as Fatal Fantasy, The Poet, Cut Color Kill, and Chameleon: Hollywood Con Queen.

This directory covers 153 public episodes, 2021 to 2026. Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order: Date, Duration, Episode, and Summary, each shown only when available. Titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”; people are sorted by surname; all sorting ignores case.

Episodes are grouped by season, earliest first, and within each group they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Appendixes reorder them by date (newest first), by title.

For current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the show’s public feed.
