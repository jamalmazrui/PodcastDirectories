Making Sense Directory

Making Sense is hosted by neuroscientist, philosopher, and author Sam Harris, who explores the mind, society, morality, religion, and current events, often in long-form conversation with a guest.

This directory is built from the show’s public RSS feed. Many full episodes are available only to subscribers at samharris.org, so the public feed carries free and partial versions; this directory reflects what the public feed contains.

Each episode’s title link plays its audio; an Episode page link follows the title when the show offers a separate page; and the metadata on record appears one kind per field, in alphabetical order, each shown only when available. Values inside a field are sorted alphabetically, ignoring case; titles ignore a leading “A”, “An”, or “The”, and people are sorted by surname.

Episodes are grouped into the categories below, and within each they are in date order, oldest first, so a show reads the way it unfolded. Three appendixes offer other orders: by date, by title, and by guest.

The show is the work of Sam Harris; for current details, the linked pages are authoritative. This directory was compiled by Jamal Mazrui from the public feed.
